## EDS Point-Scan C% Summaries

Original untreated sample:

- n = 15
- C range: 43.79-56.12 %
- Mean ± SD: 49.31 ± 4.10 %
- RSD: 8.3 %
- Interpretation: baseline C signal; extra PECVD carbon attachment/coating contribution is limited.

60 sccm CH4 / 20 sccm H2:

- n = 15
- C range: 47.08-61.02 %
- Mean ± SD: 55.76 ± 4.57 %
- RSD: 8.2 %
- Difference vs original: +6.45 percentage points
- Interpretation: moderate carbon attachment increase with uniformity close to the original; relatively controlled coating.

80 sccm CH4:

- n = 20
- C range: 46.20-95.18 %
- Mean ± SD: 69.78 ± 19.13 %
- RSD: 27.4 %
- Difference vs original: +20.47 percentage points
- Difference vs 60CH4/20H2: +14.02 percentage points
- Interpretation: strongest carbon signal and local carbon enrichment; large particle-to-particle variation suggests nonuniform coating or local thick carbon deposition.

## Preferred Comparative Conclusion

80 sccm CH4 gives the strongest carbon attachment but weaker coating uniformity. 60 sccm CH4 / 20 sccm H2 gives a smaller C increase but better consistency, so it is more favorable when uniform and controllable coating is the priority.
